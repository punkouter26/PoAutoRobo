Watch a film: a suggested note for each of your films

2026-10-22 · Each film on the lesson page has a new eye button. A click has a model look at eight frames of the film and fill in its note (a cent or two, once per film; the answer is kept). The same look happens on its own when the script is written, so the narration and the teasers follow what really happens in the film. Circled in red. The editor's-note button on the Clips step (Rewrite to answer the note) shows only when the editor has a note, which the stand-ins never have, so it is not pictured.
